Muhammad Zair · Data Analyst
Microsoft Certified · Power BI Data Analyst

I'm Muhammad Zair, a Senior Data Analyst at a reputed company with over 5 years of experience turning data into actionable insights. I specialize in data analysis, business intelligence, and automation — helping businesses make smarter decisions through clean, interactive, and user-centric dashboards.

My work combines technical depth with design thinking: every report is not only accurate but intuitive and visually engaging, built with UI/UX logic in mind so the person reading it can act without a manual. I've spent much of my career in the gaming industry, working with large event datasets on player behaviour, retention, and monetization — so alongside corporate BI I also build gaming-specific analytics.

Beyond Power BI, I build interactive HTML dashboards (custom web reports and D3 visuals for when off-the-shelf tools aren't enough) and automate the repetitive parts of reporting with Excel VBA, Power Automate, and Python — so the numbers stay fresh without manual rebuilds.
