Weekend behavior

Players spend more per day on weekends on both platforms (+18.7% Android, +21.5% iOS). Weekend-specific promos look like a high-leverage lever.

Core monetization

Remove Ads and the Pro/Elite pass bundles are the top earners on both platforms — the real monetization backbone, ahead of any cash or energy pack.

Diverging trend

Android weekday revenue is flat-to-up (+3.7%), while iOS weekday revenue is down 18.7% over the same window.

Why iOS weekdays are declining

The drop is broad-based — 27 of 40 iOS IAPs declined. Excluding seasonal bundles, the core catalog is down 33.0%, worse than the headline number — Summer Pass 2026 is currently masking a steeper underlying decline.

Leading decliners

Drop in weekly average revenue (first-3-week avg → last-3-week avg): Winter Pass 2025 −$6,074, $2750 Cash Bundle −$3,620, Manager Pass Pro + Elite −$3,318, 500 Energy Bundle −$2,754, Starter Pack −$2,436 — top 5 alone account for 49% of all decline.
